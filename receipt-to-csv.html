This converter reads printed text with a general-purpose reader, so check each value before you rely on it. Faded, crumpled or handwritten receipts are the hardest. The iPhone app uses AI to fill in the fields and usually needs fewer corrections.

How it works

  1. Add a photo. Take it straight on, in good light, with the whole receipt in frame.
  2. Check the values. The vendor, date, total and tax appear in a table. Click any cell to correct it.
  3. Download the CSV. Open it in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, or send it to your accountant.

Questions

Is my receipt uploaded anywhere?

No. The text is read by code running in your browser. The page downloads the reader and a language file, but your image stays on your device.

Does the CSV open in Excel?

Yes. The file is saved as UTF-8 with a marker Excel recognises, so accents and currency symbols display correctly.

Can I convert many receipts at once?

Yes. Choose several photos and each becomes a row. For regular use the app is faster: it scans with the camera, remembers your categories and builds reports for you.

Which total does it pick?

It looks for a line labelled total (or the equivalent in the language you chose) and ignores subtotals. If it finds none, it uses the largest amount on the receipt.
