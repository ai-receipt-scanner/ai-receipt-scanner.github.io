Choose Shoeboxed if

  • You have boxes of old paper receipts and want someone else to scan them.
  • You need it on Android or the web.
  • Several people need access to the same receipt archive.

Choose AI Receipt Scanner if

  • You track your own receipts and do not need approval workflows.
  • You want receipts stored on your phone, with no account to create.
  • You want a PDF or CSV report you can send to an accountant in one tap.
  • You would rather pay a small subscription, or nothing at all for light use.
